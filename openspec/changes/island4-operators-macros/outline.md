## Sections

### 5. Island 4: An Expressive Language (~2.5 min)

Two language features that make Nim good for scientific use: custom operators written by hand, then units and their operators generated by macros, inspired by Vindaar's unchained library. Carries K2 (metaprogramming superpower).

5.1 **Island 4** - section title on the map.
5.2 **Custom operators** - hand-written geometry: `Line` and `Plane` types over `Point` and `Vec` (a `distinct Point`), `+` joins (two points make a line, a line and a point a plane), `*` meets (a line hits a plane in a point); `Point + Point` is not a point, so the types let it mean the line through them [example].
5.3 **Where a line meets a plane** - the plane through three points, the line through two, and their meet [example]; the slide links its explainer.
5.4 **Units with a macro** - Vindaar's unchained: units checked at compile time, plain floats at run time; its `declareUnits` macro declares the SI units (left column: Gram and Meter, read from unchained's si_units.nim at build time) and adds the solar mass and the Earth mass (right column), and the macro writes the types, aliases and conversions while `*`, `/` and `+` are macros that work out the unit of the result.
5.5 **Kepler's third law** - `period(a, mass)` is `2 * PI * sqrt(a^3 / (G_Newton * mass))` for any length and any mass: the year of Mars (1.524 AU around one solar mass) and the month of the Moon (384 400 km around one Earth mass); adding a length to a mass does not compile [example].
5.6 **Rocks ahead** - with great power: macros can make code hard to read for newcomers; concepts (compile-time duck typing) were not robust until possibly recently.

## Decisions

- ADDED: Island 4 examples (Pietro, 2026-10-27): the custom operators example is geometry (points, lines, planes, with join and meet), hand-written so it fits on a slide, and the metaprogramming example is Vindaar's unchained, where macros generate units and the operators between them. Neither uses the sea metaphor, which the slides already have enough of (Pietro): the geometry is plain math, and the units are astronomical (Kepler's third law). `Vec` is a `distinct Point` (Pietro): the same representation, none of its procedures, the same trick unchained uses for units. Alternative: hand-written physical units for the easy example, closer to unchained but a repeat of the second example; a sailing theme (a shadow at sea, nautical units and hull speed), dropped.
- ADDED: The new units live in their own module (`slides/island4_units.nim`) and only `SolarMass`, `Msun`, `EarthMass` and `Mearth` are imported and exported to the deck: `declareUnits` also generates the compound SI types again, which would be ambiguous next to unchained's.

- ADDED: Code explainers (Pietro, 2026-10-27): slides have limited space for code, so an example that deserves more gets an explainer, a nimib document linked from its slide, for people who want to explore further. The first is for the custom operators example (`docs/island4_operators_explainer.html`). Alternative: longer code on the slides, or links to the source on GitHub.
